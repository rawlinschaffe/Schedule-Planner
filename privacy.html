Schedule Planner Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026
Summary: Schedule Planner is a free, non-commercial browser-based planner. It stores planner data in your browser and uses Google Calendar access only for calendar sync features you choose to use.

Who operates this app

Schedule Planner is operated personally by Rawlins Chaffe. For privacy or support questions, contact: rawlinschaffe [at] gmail [dot] com.

Information the app stores

Schedule Planner stores your planner information, such as projects, meetings, notes, categories, settings, and sync preferences, in your own browser using local storage. This information is not sent to a server operated by Schedule Planner.

Google Calendar data

If you connect Google Calendar, Schedule Planner uses Google Calendar access only to provide calendar sync features. Depending on the action you choose, the app may create or update planner meetings in your Google Calendar, read calendar lists and event color options so you can choose sync settings, and import selected calendar events into the planner.

Schedule Planner does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to third parties. Google Calendar data is used only to provide and maintain the calendar sync features shown in the app.

Where data is processed

The app runs in your browser. Google Calendar requests are made directly between your browser and Google using the Google account you authorize.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Schedule Planner does not use ads, analytics, tracking pixels, or marketing cookies.

Deleting your data

You can delete local planner data from inside the app under Settings → Clear all data. This also disconnects the app from Google in the browser. You can also remove Schedule Planner’s access from your Google Account permissions page at any time.

Data security

Because the app is browser-based and does not run its own server, your main responsibility is protecting access to your device, browser profile, and Google account. Do not use Schedule Planner on shared devices unless you are comfortable with local browser storage on that device.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Schedule Planner changes how it handles data. The updated policy will be posted on this page.

Google API disclosure

Schedule Planner’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
